## 1. Overview & Data Provenance Policy

ATLAS-Q strictly enforces transparent meteorological data provenance. Every dataset and derived metric is tagged with one of four explicit classifications:

1. **`REAL`**: Directly observed instrument telemetry (e.g. S-Band Doppler Weather Radar reflectivity volumes, INSAT-3D rapid-scan infrared brightness temperatures, IITM Damini lightning strike pulses, and Open-Meteo surface synoptic measurements).
2. **`DERIVED`**: Values calculated from raw observations (e.g. connected-component storm cell clustering, reflectivity-weighted centroids, convective area, echo top heights, flash density per 1000 km²).
3. **`PREDICTED`**: Forecast outputs generated by kinematic advection models (e.g. 0–60 minute trajectory vectors with horizontal uncertainty radii, storm decay trends, probabilistic risk tiers).
4. **`SIMULATED`**: Synthetic, deterministic meteorological scenarios generated for technical verification, repeatable evaluation, and SIH demonstration.

## 2. Ingestion Registry

| Data Stream | Primary Provider | Instrument / Gateway | Resolution & Cycle | Parameters Extracted | Provenance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Doppler Weather Radar (DWR)** | India Meteorological Department (IMD) | S-Band (2.7–2.9 GHz) & C-Band (5.6 GHz) network | 250m radial bin / 10-minute volume scan | Reflectivity ($Z$), Radial Velocity ($V$), Spectrum Width ($W$), Echo Tops | `REAL` |
| **Geostationary Satellite** | ISRO / MOSDAC | INSAT-3D / INSAT-3DR Imager & Sounder | 4 km spatial / 15-minute rapid scan | TIR-1 ($10.8\mu m$), Water Vapor ($6.7\mu m$), Cloud Top Temperature (CTT) | `REAL` |
| **Lightning Detection** | IITM Pune / Damini Network | Time-of-Arrival (TOA) Magnetic Direction Finding | 500m spatial accuracy / 1.1s continuous stream | Cloud-to-Ground (CG-, CG+), Intra-Cloud (IC), Peak Current ($kA$) | `REAL` |
| **In-Situ Surface Synoptic** | Open-Meteo & IMD AWS | Automatic Weather Stations & Radiosonde Soundings | Hourly / Realtime surface measurements | Surface Temp, Dew Point, Relative Humidity, Pressure, Wind Gusts, CAPE ($J/kg$) | `REAL` |
| **Numerical Weather Prediction** | NCMRWF / IMD WRF | High-Resolution WRF (3km) Model | 3 km grid / 3-hour forecast cycles | Environmental bulk shear (0–6km), simulated updraft helicity, boundary forcing | `REAL` |
| **Storm Detection Engine** | ATLAS-Q Detection Kernel | Connected-Component 8-Connectivity Clustering | Per radar volume scan ($32\times 32$ grid) | Cell IDs, Centroids, Area ($km^2$), $Z_{max}$, $Z_{mean}$, Echo Top ($km$) | `DERIVED` |
| **Kinematic Advection Nowcast** | ATLAS-Q Tracking Kernel | Spatial Nearest-Neighbor Centroid Matching | 10-minute cycle ($T+10m$ to $T+60m$) | Displacement ($\Delta x, \Delta y$), Speed ($km/h$), Heading ($0-360^\circ$), Uncertainty ($\sigma$) | `PREDICTED` |
| **Demonstration Scenarios** | ATLAS-Q Simulation Suite | Deterministic Hydrodynamic Scenario Grids | 7 Sequential Timesteps ($T-30m$ to $T+30m$) | Kalbaishakhi squall line, Delhi-NCR microburst, Western Ghats orographic | `SIMULATED` |
